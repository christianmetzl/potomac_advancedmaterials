⚑ Honest scope (non-negotiable)
  • No quantum-advantage claim anywhere: classical FCI/DMRG solve every instance here, including the 40q flagship (our own DMRG anchor pins it independently). We show correctness, self-certification, favourable scaling and executed hardware — never a speed or reach win.
  • Large-scale QSCI beyond 20q uses a determinant-space proxy, validated against real circuit-sampled shots at 12/16/20q.
  • The 40q absolute result is at the chemical-accuracy edge, not comfortably inside; the full 40q tensornet-mps growth run remains owed.
  • The 40q tensor-network backend ran (2.65 h, 12.06 GB) but its sampling yields ~108 determinants where ~450,000 are needed — E9 measures why, and it is a sampling-strategy limit, not GPU hours.
  • Two DFT-based decision claims were tested and withdrawn (reported, not buried): a CrO-vs-NiO ranking (inverted at larger active space) and a "B3LYP flips CrO's spin sign" claim (an SCF-convergence artifact — proper SCF gives the correct sign). The value case rests only on the CCSD(T) non-variational collapse, independent of functional/SCF/active-space.
